Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026. This policy explains what LifeOS AI collects and how we protect it.

1. What we collect

Account email, name, language preference, tasks, habits, chat history, and payment records you submit (plan, amount, transaction hash). We do not collect your private keys or seed phrase.

2. How we use it

To provide the service: organize your life, run your account, deliver AI answers, process subscription, and allow the owner to confirm payments. We never sell or rent your data.

3. Payments

Payments are made on blockchain networks (e.g., USDT TRC20). We do not custody your funds. We only display a public receiving address and record a transaction hash for verification.

4. Security

We store passwords as secure hashes and use encrypted communication. Still, no transmission is 100% secure, so we encourage strong passwords.

5. Data deletion

You may request account deletion. We will remove your personal account data; payment records may be retained for legal/accounting purposes.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner through the service.
